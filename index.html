    Good to know

    • Letters arrive by email about 30 minutes after you ask. If one hasn’t come, check your Spam or Junk folder and mark it “Not spam”.
    • Make one letter per company. Each letter carries its own reference number and QR code.
    • The first time you use a new phone or computer, we may email you a 6-digit code. This stops anyone else changing your records.
    • Making several letters? Your details stay filled in while this tab is open. Tick “Remember my details” to keep them for next time on your own phone or laptop.
    • Check spelling before you send the request. Spotted a mistake? Request a letter for the same company again within 30 minutes: the corrected letter replaces the first one and keeps the same reference.
    • Once a company accepts you, confirm your placement so the Faculty can arrange supervision visits.
    • Questions? Email the Dean’s office or Call the Dean’s office
