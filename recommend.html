Good to know

  • The Dean’s office reads each request before the letter is sent. Once approved, it is emailed to you. If it hasn’t arrived, check your Spam or Junk folder and mark it “Not spam”.
  • Write exactly what you are applying for, as the organisation names it. It is printed in the letter.
  • The letter confirms your programme and year of study and supports your application. It does not comment on your grades or conduct; for a letter about your academic work, ask a lecturer who knows it.
  • Each letter has its own reference number and QR code. The organisation can scan it to confirm the letter is genuine and approved.
  • Questions? Email the Dean’s office or Call the Dean’s office
